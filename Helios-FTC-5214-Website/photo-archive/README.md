# Helios photo archive

This folder contains copies of photos used for the Helios FTC Team 5214 website.

- `east-bay-meet-one/`: 9 photos from the November 1, 2026 BIOBUZZ league meet.
- `lakeside-intellikits/`: 6 outreach and IntelliKits photos.

These are the existing **web-optimized WebP versions**, not the original camera files. They were copied from `public/assets/` without recompression or alteration.

**Deployment:** This archive sits outside `public/`, which is the configured Vercel output directory. These archived copies are not served by the website and are not linked on any website page. The existing photos in `public/assets/` are unchanged and may still appear in the website wherever referenced.

For team privacy, do not put unreviewed photos of attendees/minors in the public assets directory.
