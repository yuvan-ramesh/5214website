OUR STORY

Since 2010.
Still building.

Team 5214 began competing in FIRST Tech Challenge in 2010. Based in Dublin, California, we've been part of a program that asks students to think like engineers, collaborate as teammates, and learn by building something real.

Today, as Helios, we bring that history into every new challenge. Seasons change, designs evolve, and the team keeps learning.
