NOVEMBER 1, 2026 · QUARRY LANE SCHOOL · DUBLIN, CA

East Bay League
Meet One.

Our first East Bay League meet of the BIOBUZZ season was a strong start for Helios. The team put Eclipse to the test and helped set an event-high alliance score of 118 points.

MATCH HIGHLIGHTS

Strong start.
Stronger together.

Helios 5214 competed at East Bay League Meet One on November 1 at Quarry Lane School. Our team brought Eclipse onto the field, worked closely with alliance partners, and put up some great scores.

One highlight was Qualification 17, where our red alliance won 118–49—the event-high score shown on the match display. In Qualification 9, Helios was on the winning blue alliance in a 99–9 match.

From preparing the robot to communicating during matches, the meet gave us a lot to build on for the rest of BIOBUZZ.
