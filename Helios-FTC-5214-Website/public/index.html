GETTING TO KNOW ROBOTICS

What are FIRST and FTC?

Robotics competitions are only part of the story. These programs help students learn engineering, teamwork, and leadership by doing real projects.

01 / THE ORGANIZATION

What is FIRST?

FIRST (For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology) is a nonprofit that introduces young people to STEM through team-based robotics programs. Students learn to solve problems, work together, and support their communities.

Learn about FIRST ↗
02 / OUR COMPETITION

What is FTC robotics?

FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) is a robotics competition for students in grades 7–12. Each season, teams design, build, and program a robot for a new game, then compete in matches with and against other teams. Outreach is part of the experience, too.
