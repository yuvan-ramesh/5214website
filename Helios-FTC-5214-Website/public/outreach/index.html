RECENT OUTREACH

More kids should get to try STEM.

On Friday, October 16, our team visited the community center at Lakeside Apartments in Concord for a STEM event with children in its youth program.

We demonstrated our competition robot, explained how we designed, built, and programmed it, and brought small STEM kits for kids to try themselves.

We especially want to reach students in underserved communities, where access to robotics clubs, equipment, and STEM activities may be limited. Everyone deserves the chance to discover what they can build.

OUR ONGOING INITIATIVE

IntelliKits

IntelliKits is our approach to introducing younger students to engineering with approachable, hands-on activities. Instead of only watching a demonstration, participants get to experiment and make something themselves.

We plan to keep sharing activities like these at community events and STEM workshops.
