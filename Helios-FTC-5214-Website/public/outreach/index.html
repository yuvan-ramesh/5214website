HELIOS IN THE COMMUNITY

Good ideas are
meant to be shared.

We build robots for competition. We share what we learn because the next great engineer could be anywhere.

WHY IT MATTERS

Making room for
more makers.

We believe an introduction to STEM can begin with something simple: a question, a small experiment, or a chance to try. Our outreach is about making that first step welcoming and hands-on.

As our projects develop, this space will document the activities, lessons, and people behind them.
